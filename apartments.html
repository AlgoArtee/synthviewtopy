Lux Umbrarum Pool Commons

A quiet ripple. An open book. Sunlight tracing the tiles.

A spacious sunlit pool hall with arched garden windows, ivory tiles and pearl-and-platinum loungers facing the water from every side. Swim in the aquamarine basin or climb the brass diving board and jump in with a splash. Long pearl pod seats hang from metallic double-helix suspensions beside antique books and a grand brass clock.
