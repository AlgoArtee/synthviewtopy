**Remaining limitations are visible and should remain documented:**

- Wet-road reflections still form unusually regular longitudinal bands. Broad, smoothly bounded puddles read as simplified effects rather than naturally broken reflections across uneven asphalt.
- Foggy night suppresses the central street and distant figures heavily. Near signs remain readable, but additional subtle atmospheric light scattering would give the darkness more depth.
- Autumn cues are understated. Small leaves are difficult to distinguish in the reviewed full-resolution compositions; these stills do not independently establish their movement or abundance.
- Existing bedding remains smoother and more uniformly shaded than the rumpled cotton reference. Repeated façade lighting patterns and simplified traffic also limit photographic realism.
- Expanded controls occupy much of the phone view. At 844×390, the window/audio panel has a particularly short visible area, leaving sound controls below the shown portion. Their scrolling accessibility is a functional matter not proven by the still.
- In the storage-disabled 390×844 view, the accurate session-only notice appears alongside “stored on this device” and “Saved on this device” wording. That generic copy should reflect the fallback state consistently.

These are residual quality and clarity issues, not rejection-level visual defects for the current scope.
