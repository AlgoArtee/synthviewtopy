**Remaining limitations**

- The new cushion faces are unusually taut and similar. Broad quiet surfaces help the artwork, but more individual corner collapse and local compression would improve the impression of loose filling.
- Close views reveal fine, fairly parallel side creases. Some read as shallow incised lines rather than folds produced by fabric tension. Shorter, less regular creases with softer transitions would improve textile realism.
- The low view shows a narrowly tapered lower edge on the nearest small cushion. It has convincing overall volume, but a slightly broader compressed contact patch would look softer.
- Strong, even illumination suppresses subtle cotton texture, particularly in clear weather. The material is credibly matte but still visibly computer-rendered.
- The phone capture demonstrates readable artwork on the central cushion; it does not show the entire seven-cushion arrangement simultaneously.

These limitations prevent a justified 9/10 textile-realism score. They do not prevent acceptance at the requested minimum. The existing large-pillow grooves and pointed lower corners belong to the unchanged base and have not been treated as newly introduced cushion defects.
