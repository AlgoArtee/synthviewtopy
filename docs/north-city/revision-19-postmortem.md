# Covered balcony and active weather plaza

The user authorized three additions to the existing apartment: an upper-floor ceiling above its L-shaped balcony, weather across the balcony outlook, and more light and activity in the circuit plaza. The final implementation keeps a continuous 3.5 m underside above the walking route and places precipitation beyond the 5.08 m roof-edge boundary. Walking residents, trains, moving circuit packets and localized lantern light animate the plaza.

The first implementation exposed three useful defects. Geometry support checks caught walkers hovering above the physical slab; testing the transformed shoe vertices, including their pitch and size variation, resolved this. Disposal-event probes caught ten instanced meshes that released shared geometry/materials but left their per-instance buffers allocated. Finally, dense fog made black rectangles conspicuous: raycasts identified steam and glow sprites omitted from the opaque ambient-occlusion exclusion. Classifying transparent sprites alongside transparent meshes removed those artifacts.
