# Four times of day and atmospheric neon

Time and weather are now independent selections. The six standard weather conditions are grouped under the selected Night, Morning, Day or Evening; four retained Dark Academia presets occupy their own group. Their local labels no longer encode a conflicting time. Stable weather IDs preserve the existing sound library and precipitation behavior.

Cached combined profiles drive the sky, fog color, hemisphere and ground light, sun/moon position, window bounce, indoor fixtures, reflection environment, exposure and bloom. Morning light comes from the east, evening light from the west, with a higher daytime sun and cool low night illumination. Dark Academia uses warm earthy morning/day palettes. Actual shadow-camera coverage extends to the new light positions, and lighting changes refresh the reflection probe and shadow maps.

Distant neon is handled separately from opaque surfaces. Window and sign emission maps mask their luminous pixels. A shared material hook retains normal geometry fog and adds only the emissive contribution lost to the stronger body extinction. The existing bloom pass spreads the surviving light cores. Actual WebGL fixture pixels confirm that night emission brightens while the unlit facade remains at the fog color. Profile multipliers preserve animation pulses without compounding on repeated selections.

The time/weather panel and aperture/audio panel share one bounded scroll area, keeping every control reachable without horizontal overflow. Time changes keep the weather and selected audio track; weather changes keep the time. The existing balcony door and window still determine indoor sound admission.

Validation and the independent scoped visual review are recorded in the revision-21 validation and review files. Historical whole-city fidelity and prior review outcomes remain separate. No scene layout, asset provenance, paid generation, external export, commit or deployment is part of this revision.
