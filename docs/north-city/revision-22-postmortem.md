# Wet-weather daylight and distant neon polish

Daytime rain and storm now receive brighter diffuse sky and window light. Rain uses a lighter blue-gray sky, while storm stays darker with its existing heavy precipitation. This override applies only to the standard rain/storm Day combinations; precipitation physics, other times and the Dark Academia palettes remain intact.

Non-fog nights retain bright light cores with much smaller bloom strength and radius and a higher bloom threshold. Fog and Dark Academia fog retain their earlier soft halos. Baked blur was removed from balcony sign titles, leaving weather-dependent softness to the runtime light treatment.

Distant architectural accents use seven hues with bands, frames, strips and dashes. Billboard lettering and occasional office-window groups add warm, mint, violet and rose accents to the existing cyan city. Nine balcony and five north-city lights have independent brief dim/recovery events, on separate 18–41-second cycles. These are bounded emission changes rather than synchronized bright flashes. Reduced motion restores steady emission. Geometry and textures remain static during animation.
