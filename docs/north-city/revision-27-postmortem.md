# Revision 27 outcome

The new Plaza data stream speed control ranges from paused to 3× normal in 0.1× steps, saves locally, and integrates speed changes without moving packets to a new phase. Trains and walking residents keep their own timing. Reduced motion holds data streams steady and does not accumulate catch-up movement.

Both distant city views have supported uplink spires, open lanterns, circular technical decks, sloped crowns and paired stacks. Eight shared graphic billboard designs and mounted facade circuit accents add cyan, magenta, violet, mint and warm landmarks. These are static geometry and textures, batched by material, with no new real-time lights. Emission responds to the existing time/weather profiles; opaque architectural wash retains ordinary body fog. Supported horizontal crown surfaces receive snow.

Round 1 scored 7.7/10, not accepted: graphic signs and the speed UI read clearly, but thin, nearly dark roof forms competed with compressed window noise. Larger supported masses, thicker local accents, circular halo decks, restrained structural emission and solid rooftop machinery cladding address those findings. Actual living-window controls remain intact. Round 2 accepted 8.1/10 for this feature scope; earlier whole-city and feature grades remain unchanged.

Validation: production build, 12 stream-clock regressions, 47 actual-room checks, 16 native captures and the original bundled browser client pass. Native speed ratios, pause, phase continuity, independent trains, reload persistence, keyboard/mobile use and reduced motion were checked. Clear night, evening, morning, day, fog and snow captures are retained. Both actual skyline groups have finite geometry, totaling 43700 triangles, and zero additional real-time lights. The retained native 8-second clip was not continuously auditioned; screenshot review and measured timing are separate evidence.

Live user window preferences were preserved at 35% shadows, 19% illuminated and 54% TV; Day/Rain and 1× stream speed were retained. No paid assets, export, commit or deployment. Repeated base-tower architecture and natural distant obscuration in fog remain limits.
