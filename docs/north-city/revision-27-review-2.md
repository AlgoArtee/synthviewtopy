## Findings

**The dimensional forms now survive normal-size viewing.** The northern skyline plainly separates a warm open frame at the left, a sloped roof outline beside it, paired angular spires left of center, and a cyan ringed crown near the center. The taller right-hand tower has distinct pink technical stacks rather than a noisy compressed continuation of small window rows. In the southern view, the ringed structures, paired vertical stacks and broad sloped crown have different profiles and visible height. Their dark supporting masses meet the towers below, and the luminous bands read as architectural components rather than isolated dots. This directly addresses round 1's failed criterion.

**Some of the improvement reaches the main balcony framing.** A framed roof landmark sits above the central mint billboard, curved cyan/pink crown portions appear at the upper edge, and long mounted facade traces add large vertical accents on either side. A viewer can identify the changed light vocabulary in the full plaza composition without first switching to a close inspection camera. The northern and southern production viewpoints reveal the complete roof silhouettes more clearly; the main view still crops some of the highest structures, which is a normal framing limit rather than the only way those structures are presented.

**The new hierarchy is closer to the reference's useful qualities.** Larger cyan, mint, pink and occasional warm features now stand apart from the ordinary pale window grid. The graphic faces, vertical facade accents and different roof profiles provide several distant landmarks while the foreground Data Mall and circuit plaza remain the main visual focus. The solid, quieter roof tiers reduce the earlier visual noise and give the neon forms a clearer base. The result is an incremental stylized interpretation of the reference, not a reproduction of its photorealistic detail or its broader city layout.

**The rail and existing composition remain readable.** In the native sequence the pale train moves from right to left along the guideway above the mall without being hidden by the added structures. The new signs and facade traces occupy the background rather than obscuring the retail frontage, plaza or balcony railing. Through the north window, the distant vertical accents and ringed structure add depth while the closer Nova, Luna, Cafe and holographic figures retain their hierarchy. No obvious new floating board, displaced tower edge, clipping plane or rail blockage appears in the inspected frames.

**The stream control remains visually successful.** The desktop and mobile panels retain a clear Plaza data stream speed label, readable multiplier and distinct track. The native night samples show bright packets at different positions along the fixed foreground trunk and plaza branches. The board stays steadily illuminated and the selected packet positions change without making the entire pavement flash together. The stills support readable packet activity; they are not used to infer exact speed response or uninterrupted movement between every sampled instant.

**Weather softening is preserved.** In clear day and evening the new features are less contrasty than at night, but their larger shapes remain discernible. Morning cloud, evening fog and snow progressively obscure the distant towers while leaving the nearby facade and boards clearer. Faint crown bands remain visible through that atmosphere, without turning every distant roof into a bright outline. Snow retains visible accumulation on the nearby roofs and ledges. The correction therefore improves clear-night readability without erasing the existing weather depth.
