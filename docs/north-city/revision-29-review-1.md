## Findings

**The mall reads as a substantially taller building while retaining its identity and frontage.** Three additional rows of modeled gallery bays and distinct floor bands rise above the original ground-level shopfronts. The upper main sign, lower department boards and illuminated glazing clearly identify the larger mass as Data Mall. Its edges remain upright and floor bands level. The requested exact change from 11.2 m to 33.6 m is established by geometry checks rather than guessed from screen pixels. Visually, the increased height is unmistakable and the plaza remains visible below it. The main camera now devotes most of its upper composition to the mall, an expected consequence of tripling this nearby building.

**The raised transit is visible after the deck correction.** In `mall-hybrid-night.png`, the pale three-car train is recognizable on the near rising curve above the roof. `raised-connected-rail.png` shows separated car bodies and the narrow supported guideway across the mall. The southern view exposes the continuous curve and tall columns, with another visible train segment at the left. The native sequence also retains a recognizable train near the right-hand elevated segment in the eight-second frame. This is a concrete improvement over the earlier ungraded wide deck, where mostly cyan roof outlines survived. The train remains small and some portions of the loop naturally obscure more of its lower body when viewed from below, but it is no longer consistently hidden by the slab. No obvious route termination, detached car or mall intersection appears in the final stills.

**The reduced modeled district and image city form a coherent layered composition.** Foreground retail and pedestrians supply clear near depth; the retained towers create an intermediate silhouette; the more detailed industrial city extends behind them. Lowering some intermediate towers reveals the image city's varied spires, mechanical facade detail and mounted signs. The distinction is particularly clear in the northern, southern and balcony-return views. The final stills do not show an obvious rectangular sky edge or major seam between image sectors. The image background is understandably flatter and has different fine detail from the simplified modeled towers. Continuous walking parallax and every extreme camera direction were not visually auditioned here; this acceptance is for the supplied production viewpoints.

**The reference is represented as inspiration, with a substantial remaining lighting difference.** The new background captures its varied industrial architecture, tall irregular silhouettes and a mixture of cyan, violet, pink and warm graphic accents. It is appreciably more detailed than the former repeated far-tower field. It does not reproduce the reference's dense, brightly populated magenta/cyan advertising or its luminous urban haze. The final clear-night image city remains comparatively quiet and blue, with fewer dominant neon landmarks. The lower intermediate masses and stronger mounted screens improve readability enough for this scoped hybrid transition, but the reference's rich night-light hierarchy is only partially matched. This limitation is a material reason for the threshold score rather than a higher realism score.

**The window extremes remain visually consequential.** Off, steady and TV/shadow images show conspicuous differences in the modeled residential openings while commercial boards and the mall retain their separate illumination. The exposed image city also gains small warm or cool points at the steady and active settings; these remote rooms resolve mainly as lights, not readable interior silhouettes. The nearby selected windows show broad colored illumination and shadow divisions. Static extremes cannot establish exact percentages, independent selection, every frame of TV motion, or the absence of every residual pixel at zero. Those assertions belong to the parent's shared-helper and actual-render checks. The independently editable screen instances likewise require the functional editing check; editable ownership cannot be inferred merely from a board's appearance.

**Time and weather remain coherent across the modeled and image portions.** Day clear, rain and storm keep the mall readable through progressively heavier precipitation and atmospheric attenuation. Fog appropriately obscures the distant image structures, and snow includes visible accumulation on the nearer roof edges and ledges. Morning is pale and warm near lit surfaces; Academia morning and day introduce an evident warm cast and airborne leaves while maintaining the same buildings and route. The background does not remain a conspicuous fixed night photograph against the bright daytime sky. In the strongest fog and snow, individual far structures are intentionally difficult to judge.

**The apartment and balcony views remain integrated.** The mobile framing retains the mall's stacked galleries, plaza and raised curve, although it naturally crops the long mall sign and neighboring blocks. The north-window canyon, balcony ceiling, return corridor and interior return show no obvious new clipping or intrusion from the far-city planes. Plaza walkers, drones and circuit packets occupy different positions in the native samples. Those frames support ongoing scene activity but do not establish continuous playback smoothness.

## Performance and scope limits

The far modeled tower reduction is a functional implementation fact, not evidence of a proportional FPS gain. The final runtime record reports **684 draw submissions and 2,158,603 submitted triangles per sampled whole-room frame**, including shared reflection and postprocessing passes. This review does not claim those totals are an improvement over a controlled baseline, that the whole scene is inexpensive, or that a still image demonstrates frame-rate stability. The much taller detailed mall also adds work. Any user-facing performance conclusion should use the separate measurements and preserve these limits.

This acceptance covers the taller mall, visible raised transit, layered image-backed composition and inspected weather/control integration. It is not a new whole-city realism acceptance or a claim of close photorealistic reproduction of the reference. The upper mall bays are repetitive and relatively dark, remote TV detail resolves as colored points, and the far neon palette remains sparser than the reference. The implementation meets the scoped visual threshold while retaining those explicit limitations.
