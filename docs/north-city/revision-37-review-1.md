The ten stands fill the requested side spaces at a believable size relative to the pedestrians and shops. The five-per-side arrangement preserves the central circuit composition, main shop facades and visible circulation gaps. Day, night/rain and snow views show the stands integrated into the existing atmosphere. Counters, shelves, display cases, suspended parts and merchandise are dimensional; no obvious floating-stock defect appears in this capture set.

The references' distinctive market structures and material character are not yet carried far enough into the result. All ten share essentially the same clean rectangular shell, identical flat sign profile and broad pale counter. From the balcony this reads as a row of matching kiosks; at close range several goods read as simple demonstration primitives against much more detailed photographic backdrops. This limits the realism score despite the sound layout.

## Prioritized corrections before round 2

1. **Vary the large structural silhouettes.** Give the food/produce stalls a projecting or pitched canopy, fabric an angled hood and visible hanging rack, and repair an open gantry with a lower/front-mounted sign. Tech stalls can retain rigid framed displays. Three or four structural families would carry the reference differences at balcony distance without requiring ten independent high-detail assets. Keep existing clearances and footprints.
2. **Make the work surfaces and fronts feel used and functional.** The large uniformly pale counter areas and repeated clean black cabinet fronts dominate the detail shots. Use darker brushed metal or worn wood where appropriate, visible panel/seam/fastener detail, a few utility boxes or price cards, and denser secondary stock in the exposed counter areas. The repair and fabric stalls especially need this. The product shapes can remain simplified for this viewing distance; a photorealistic replica is not required.
3. **Resolve the conspicuous backdrop/foreground mismatch and fabric cloth.** Large photo panels duplicate the simplified foreground goods, most noticeably the fruit, vinyl and repair stalls. Confine photos to clearly framed merchandise screens/posters, or reduce their prominence behind real shelving; avoid visible white reference-background fragments. In `detail-fabric.png`, the loom shows thin threads with no evident sheet of cloth. Root reports the cloth plane faces away from this viewing side; ensure the cloth reads from the customer-facing side. These are visible presentation issues, not reasons to redo the plaza.

No relocation blocker is visible. Existing lamps obscure a portion of the supplemental fabric/myco/vinyl views, but do not establish a stall collision and are not scored as a new scene defect. Choose a clearer supplemental fabric camera for the next review so cloth and rack changes can be assessed.

## Reference interpretation and limitations

The noodle pots/bowls, three cyberware cases, holographic fish, mushroom trays, spice bottles, textile spools/garments, memory tubes, retro monitors/records, fruit crates/lanterns and hanging repair parts all communicate their intended category. Neon accents and dark frames fit the city. The fruit boat reference can reasonably become a ground stall here; a boat or canal is not required. The supplied reference set contains ten stand images, although the request says references 3 through 13.

The close views are supplemental QA camera positions in the same running scene, not user-accessible street navigation. Primary placement evidence comes from the public balcony viewpoints. Captures establish visible integration, not exhaustive collision or animation behavior. The reported nine native checks and source-hash stability were not used to inflate the visual score.
