## Visible findings

- Both figures have recognizable heads, slender upper bodies and floor-length gowns. Neither cyan nor pink retains the earlier rigid leg-and-platform mannequin appearance.
- The dresses contain fine luminous lines and transparent layered surfaces. Their brightness preserves internal structure; they do not become opaque glowing cones.
- In the six-frame contact sheet, the cyan hem alternately sweeps outward and gathers toward the body. Its trailing veil changes independently of the central gown. The pink gown also changes outline, at different apparent phases.
- Separated motion captures show that these changes are not explained solely by rotating the entire figure. The upper-body orientation changes modestly while the lower dress edges and trailing contours change more substantially.
- Both heads remain below and visually separated from the upper bridge in the reviewed main and sampled views.
- The cyan-right/pink-left arrangement is retained, and both remain legible in the narrow mobile city composition.
- Clear and storm views retain the ghost silhouettes. Snow reduces contrast considerably. Heavy fog makes the figures extremely faint or effectively lost against the pale background.
- The floating chair, circular white rug, bedside lamp, bed and arched city composition remain visually consistent with the preceding accepted revision in the reviewed interior views.

## Motion evidence and limits

**Direct WebM playback was not performed by this reviewer.** The review used the six-frame cropped contact sheet, wall-time-separated full screenshots and recorded animation state. The flowing quality and temporal continuity are therefore inferred from samples. This report does not claim to have independently verified uninterrupted normal-speed smoothness, frame pacing, every cloth intersection, fine-line flicker or all loop transitions.

The supplied evidence includes ghost-motion.webm and ghost-motion-close.webm. hologram-results.json describes the capture as normal speed without time acceleration and records a clip duration of 26.208 seconds. This duration and playback description are metadata, not a claim of direct playback.

The recorded before/after state spans simulation times 4.619 to 31.118 seconds. Both figures have changing cloth probes, yaw and sway. For example, the cyan cloth probe changes from [0.14409, 0.16018, 0.22726] to [0.11423, 0.10176, 0.31274], while the pink probe changes from [0.0523, 0.10951, 0.17917] to [-0.01539, 0.14507, 0.25673]. These values support actual cloth deformation, together with the visibly changing silhouettes. They do not independently establish aesthetic quality.

The sampled shapes are consistent with a slow, flowing effect. The subjective description “hypnotic” is plausible for that motion direction, but is not treated as a measurable test result.

## Remaining limitations

1. **Cable occlusion:** Several dark cables cross both figures around the torso and waist. The full gowns are visible, but this overlap hides some filigree and weakens the purity of their silhouettes.
2. **Fine-detail readability:** Threads and translucent edges can look granular at normal city-view distance. Their complete temporal stability cannot be established from the sampled stills.
3. **Abstract anatomy:** Heads and arms remain simplified luminous forms. This suits a ghostly abstraction but does not provide photographic human detail.
4. **Weather contrast:** The ghosts become faint in snow and nearly disappear in heavy fog. They are best presented in the default rain/night atmosphere or clearer weather.
5. **Previously documented scene limits remain:** The hard-outlined blue road puddle, patterned wet-road light, modular facades, simplified cars and bridge pedestrians, relatively simple bed folds and fine/granular carpet pile remain visible. This focused revision does not claim to solve those issues.

None of these observations requires another formal iteration merely to exhaust the review allowance. The current evidence reaches the requested 8/10 threshold.
