## What works

The extra volume and sideways arrangement are clear in `bed-wide.png` and `sleep.png`. The duvet gathers toward the TV wall, replacing the earlier straight full-width turnback with a less regular arrangement.

`duvet-drape.png` and `lounge.png` establish that fabric extends beyond the open foot of the mattress. The blanket therefore communicates excess size through visible draping, rather than relying solely on reported dimensions.

The four upright pillows remain against the wall opposite the TV. Their contact appears plausible in `pillows.png`, and the two low pillows leave the central panorama substantially unobstructed.

The inspected ceiling view retains continuous shading. The clear chair, lamp and circular rug remain coherent in the lounge view.

## Changes needed before acceptance

### 1. Make the hanging foot read as loose filled cloth

In `lounge.png`, the hanging face forms a broad, nearly vertical panel with a very straight lower edge. `duvet-drape.png` shows a large smooth bend into that face. Together they resemble an upholstered cover more than a duvet falling under gravity.

Introduce two or three unequal, rounded folds with different hanging depths. Vary the thickness locally and taper the filled edge toward its seam. Keep a continuous supported transition over the mattress edge; avoid replacing the flat face with evenly spaced corrugations.

### 2. Soften the angular edge near the east pillows

`bed-wide.png` shows a pronounced ridge beside the pillow row, with several sharp, scalloped transitions. These look more like sculpted geometry than compressed cotton.

Round and blend those transitions into the surrounding fabric. Retain local compression beneath the pillows without creating a continuous raised barrier alongside them.

### 3. Break up the long west-side ridges

The TV-side gathering expresses the requested direction, but some folds extend as long, smooth ridges. Shorter, unequal gathers that taper and merge would better suggest fabric pushed casually sideways.

Preserve quieter areas between folds. The aim is believable variation in weight and compression, not uniformly dense wrinkles.
