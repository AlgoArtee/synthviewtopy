The hanging foot is visibly improved in `lounge.png`. Its lower edge now varies in height, and its thickness tapers toward the seam. This breaks up the previous straight, upholstered-panel appearance. The blanket’s transition over the mattress remains continuous.

The angular repeated pockets beside the east-wall pillows are absent in `bed-wide.png` and `pillows.png`. The smoother compression strip integrates the blanket with the pillow row without the previous raised, scalloped barrier.

The TV-side gathering is shorter and less repetitive. `bed-wide.png` and `sleep.png` show several unequal raised areas rather than uninterrupted parallel ridges. The duvet still clearly communicates extra material pushed toward that side.

The four upright pillows remain on the wall opposite the TV. Their lower edges appear supported. `panorama.png` confirms that the central city view remains substantially clear.

These visible changes justify the increase from 7.9 to 8.1. Technical validation did not contribute points.

## Remaining limitations

- The duvet’s broad centre remains very smooth. Its loft is more convincing at folds and edges than across the main surface.
- The hanging face and large hem wave still look somewhat sculpted. Smaller variations in compression and gravity-driven creasing would bring them closer to natural cotton.
- The west-side gathers are improved but retain a clean, simplified appearance compared with the original rumpled bedding photograph.
- Pillow faces still have limited stuffing deformation and relatively uniform surfaces.
- The visible city retains repeated facade modules, simplified figures and vehicles, and patterned road reflections.

The accepted result is a plausible real-time interpretation of the requested oversized soft duvet. It is not indistinguishable from photographed bedding.
