# Bed-window holograms

Three original character artworks generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator on 2026-10-14 for this project. All depict fictional adult Japanese women. Original generated PNG files are preserved unchanged. Transparent background was requested. The renderer uses their alpha and additive emission so black shading does not appear as a rectangular backdrop.

## fan-dancer.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high-quality transparent character artwork for a monumental holographic projection in a realistic 3D cyberpunk city. Primary request: An adult Japanese woman, about 30, a graceful fan dancer in a full-length contemporary silk kimono with a wrapped obi, spacious long sleeves, geometric cherry blossom embroidery and a sculptural dark updo with two delicate luminous hairpins. She holds one open Japanese folding fan beside her shoulder, other hand extended naturally. Peaceful confident expression; balanced elegant standing pose, body facing almost front, face clearly visible. Style: realistic facial anatomy and finely rendered clothing, cinematic digital art, luminous hologram with dimensional shading. Whole figure built from icy neon blue, cyan and soft neon pink light; visible delicate circuit filaments and contour light in fabric, a few sparse pixel sparks at edges. Dark translucent blue interior shading retains detail in face and clothing, bright cyan and pink rim illumination. Tall portrait composition, full body from hair or parasol top to feet visible, small clean margins, no cropping. Isolate the SINGLE character and her handheld prop on genuinely transparent alpha background. No ground, no rectangular display, no text, no city or scenery, no white background, no large glow cloud. Do not include any additional person. Preserve elegant adult proportions and realistic face. The result should remain readable when projected large against dark buildings.

Generation setting: `transparent_background: true`.
