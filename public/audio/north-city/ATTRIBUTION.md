# North City ambience

“Cyberpunk rain ambience (rain and city noises stem)” by **Rmshh**.

Source: https://freesound.org/people/Rmshh/sounds/743999/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The publicly offered HQ MP3 preview is stored as `cyberpunk-city-ambience.mp3`. Runtime adaptations: moderate RMS/peak normalization, a two-second loop crossfade, volume fades, distance attenuation and low-pass filtering to follow the apartment window. No endorsement is implied. Source metadata and SHA256 are in `source.json`. Attribution is also available beside the apartment's sound controls.
